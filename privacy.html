Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: Eikora is built so your work stays on your phone.

You can use every editing feature without an account, and then we collect nothing at all. If you create an account, we keep only what that account needs: your email, a user ID, your subscription status and — only if you turn on cloud backup — copies of the projects you choose to back up.

No ads. No analytics or tracking tools. We never sell your data or use your photos to train AI.

Who we are

Eikora (“the app”) is provided by Eikora (“we”, “us”), based in Melbourne, Australia. We are responsible for the information described here. Questions or requests: mohammadrezaie565@gmail.com.

Your photos and videos

Photos and videos you import, take with the app’s cameras, or edit are processed on your device. Editing, looks, eras, effects, cut-outs and subject detection (finding the person in a photo) all run on your phone; the images are not sent to us or to anyone else for this.

  • Photo library: the app only reads the photos and videos you pick. Exports are saved to your library when you tap Save to Photos.
  • Camera and microphone: used only while you are taking a photo or recording a video in the app. Sound is recorded with your videos.
  • Capture dates: the app reads a photo’s own date (from its file) to order Reels and show date stamps you choose. This stays in your project.

Your projects are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, unless you have backed them up.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run accounts and subscriptions. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase — account sign-in, the database for subscription status and saved items, and storage for cloud backups. Data is stored in the Asia-Pacific region.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status from Apple (receives your user ID and purchase history, not your payment details).
  • Apple — the App Store processes payments and Sign in with Apple; Google — if you choose Sign in with Google. Their own privacy policies apply to those services.

We share information only with these providers, or if required by law. We never sell or rent personal information.

Storage outside your country

Your account and backup data are stored with Supabase in the Asia-Pacific region, and RevenueCat and Apple may process subscription information in other countries, including the United States. We use providers that protect data under their own security and privacy commitments.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process account, purchase and backup information because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract), and to keep the service secure (legitimate interests). We do not process your data for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and saved items: until you delete your account.
  • Cloud backups: until you delete a backup or your account.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for your subscription and as required by tax and accounting law.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app at Profile › Account › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your saved items and all your cloud backups. Projects on your phone stay there until you delete them or the app. Subscriptions are managed by Apple and are not cancelled by deleting your account — cancel them in your App Store account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and its Australian Privacy Principles, the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, or object to or restrict its use. Email mohammadrezaie565@gmail.com and we will respond within the time the law requires. You can also complain to your local data protection authority — in Australia, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Backups are stored privately: only your signed-in account can read your own backups.

Children

Eikora is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
